How to find programs and events at Orangeville Public Library. Programs are free. Dates, times and registration links are in the events calendar at calendar.orangevillelibrary.ca. Many children's programs are drop-in; most adult and Makerspace programs need registration. You can sign up for the library newsletter on the website, or call 519-941-0610.

Free children's programs at Orangeville Public Library in fall 2026. No registration is needed. Baby storytime, for babies up to 18 months: Wednesdays 1 to 1:30 p.m. at the Mill Street Library, and Fridays 10:30 to 11 a.m. at the Alder Street Library. Storytime for ages 5 and under: Tuesdays 10:30 to 11 a.m. at Alder Street, and Thursdays 10:30 to 11 a.m. at Mill Street. After School Club, for ages 7 to 12: the last Monday of the month, 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. at Mill Street. These run from early September to late November 2026. Recorded storytimes are on the library's YouTube channel.

Free programs for teens aged 13 to 17 at Orangeville Public Library in fall 2026. The Teen Advisory Group meets on the second Friday of the month, 4 to 5:30 p.m. at the Alder Street Library, and helps plan library activities. Teen Chronicles, a book club run by teens, meets on the fourth Friday of the month, 4 to 5 p.m. at Alder Street. Teens earn community service hours for both, and one hour for each online book review they submit. Teen Book Stash is a monthly box of books and treats; sign-up opens in September.

Free programs for adults at Orangeville Public Library in fall 2026. After Hours Write Club is a writing workshop every other Tuesday at 7 p.m. at the Mill Street Library; register online. Orange thREADs is a drop-in stitching and book chat group on the third Tuesday of the month at 1 p.m. Experts in the Library is a monthly talk series at Mill Street on topics such as money and technology; registration is required. The library also hosts several book clubs, some with a waiting list, and author evenings shared with other Dufferin County libraries.

Makerspace programs at the Alder Street Library in October 2026. Laser Engraving 101 is on Tuesday, October 13 at 6:30 p.m. Heat Press Everything is on Thursday, October 22 at 6:30 p.m.; bring a shirt, sweater or other fabric, and registration opens October 8. All about Coasters is on Tuesday, October 27 at 6:30 p.m., and registration opens October 13. Each program is for ages 14 and up, is limited to 6 people, and needs registration through the library's events calendar.
